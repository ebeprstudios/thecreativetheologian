What Does It Mean to Create
from a Place of Surrender?

A conversation with Mia Crews — poet, artist, and the first creative featured on the network.

cover photograph
Erica Ehiwe Founder · The Creative Theologian

Mia Crews writes from graveyards. Not literally — but the images in her poetry carry the weight of things that have died, the quiet of things waiting to be raised. She sat with us and talked about craft, calling, and what it means to hold beauty and sorrow in the same breath.

We met on a Tuesday morning, the kind of overcast day that feels like it's waiting for something. Mia arrived early, tea already in hand, a worn copy of Lamentations on the table. That detail felt right.
