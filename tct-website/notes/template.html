2 Corinthians 4:17

The Weight of Glory
Is Not Heavy

What if the burden isn't the problem — what if it's pointing to something permanent?

cover photograph

"For our light and momentary troubles are achieving for us an eternal glory that far outweighs them all."

2 Corinthians 4:17 · NIV

Paul doesn't say the trouble is light. He says it's producing something. The weight isn't the point — what it's achieving is the point. That's a completely different way to hold suffering.

Key Point

Glory is not the absence of weight — it's what the weight was always moving toward. The eternal outweighs the momentary, but only if we stay in it long enough to see.

the creative application

This is a good spot to connect the scripture to the creative life specifically. How does this apply to making things — to craft, to calling, to the work?

Questions for Reflection
